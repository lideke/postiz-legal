# Privacy Policy — Postiz perso

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

Postiz perso is a self-hosted, personal tool used exclusively by its owner. It has no other users.

## Data collected
When the owner connects a TikTok account, the application receives and stores:
- OAuth access and refresh tokens;
- basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar URL, follower and video statistics);
- the videos and captions the owner chooses to publish.

## How data is used
This data is used only to publish the owner's own content to the owner's TikTok account and to display basic account statistics. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## Storage
Data is stored in a private database on infrastructure controlled by the owner. It is not transferred to any other service, except to TikTok when content is published.

## Retention and deletion
Data is kept while the TikTok account remains connected. Disconnecting the account in the application deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.
